Who can run a fundraiser?
Schools, sports teams, churches, clubs, dance studios and other community organizations — if you have a group and a goal, we can help.
How much does our group keep?
Your group keeps a share of every bag sold. The exact profit share depends on the flavors, quantities and delivery plan, so we confirm it with you before you start selling.
How long does a fundraiser take?
The selling window is built around your group's timeline. Tell us your dates and we'll map out a plan around your delivery or pickup day.
Is there a minimum order?
Minimums depend on the flavors and quantities. Ask for the current terms when you request your kit and we'll lay it out clearly.
