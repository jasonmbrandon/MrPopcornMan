Good questions

Before you book

How far ahead should I book?
Send us your date as soon as you have it and we'll check availability. If your event is coming up soon, call and we'll see what we can do.
Do you need power at the venue?
We pop on-site and will confirm the setup details for your venue when we quote. Tell us where you're hosting and whether there's power and vehicle access, and we'll take it from there.
Can I add flavors or extra time?
Yes. Any package can be built out with more flavors, extra service time or a bigger guest count. We'll price it clearly before you commit.
Do you make bagged favors?
Ask about bagged popcorn favors for your event — they're a popular add-on for showers, weddings and corporate gifts. We'll confirm quantities and flavors in your quote.
Where do you travel?
We're based in Los Angeles and regularly serve the San Fernando Valley, South Bay and nearby communities. Send your city or zip and we'll confirm travel for your event.
