Hi, I'm Andre

Popcorn, the way it should be

Mr. Popcorn Man is Andre — a Los Angeles popcorn maker with a truck, a kettle and a simple promise: fresh popcorn, made by hand, served right where the party is.

Every batch starts with the classics you grew up with, then goes somewhere fun. Kettle corn, cheddar, caramel, strawberry shortcake, ghost pepper white cheddar, blueberry caramel cake — the menu is built for a crowd with different cravings.

You'll find the truck at birthdays, school fundraisers, church events, tailgates and office lunches all over Los Angeles. When it's gone, it's gone — because it's popped fresh, not pulled off a shelf.
